\section*{Céa's Lemma for $H^1_0$: Error bound by best approximation}

Proof.

Let $V = H^1_0(\Omega)$ with the norm $\|\cdot\|_1$, and let
$a: V \times V \to \mathbb{R}$ be a bounded, coercive bilinear form and
$f \in V'$. Consider the continuous problem: find $y \in V$ such that
$a(y, v) = f(v)$ for all $v \in V$. By the Lax--Milgram theorem, there exists
a unique $y \in V$ provided $a$ is bounded and coercive and $f \in V'$.

Let $V_h \subset V$ be a finite-dimensional subspace and let $y_h \in V_h$
be the unique solution of the discrete problem
$a(y_h, w_h) = f(w_h)$ for all $w_h \in V_h$; this is the well-posed discrete
problem by applying the Lax--Milgram theorem on the finite-dimensional space
$V_h$.

Define the error $e = y - y_h$. Galerkin orthogonality then holds:
\[
a(e, w_h) = a(y - y_h, w_h) = f(w_h) - a(y_h, w_h) = f(w_h) - f(w_h) = 0
\]
for all $w_h \in V_h$.

Using $e$, we compute
\[
a(e, e) = a(e, e - v_h) + a(e, v_h).
\]
Since $a(e, v_h) = 0$ for all $v_h \in V_h$, we obtain
\[
a(e, e) = a(e, e - v_h)
\]
for any $v_h \in V_h$.

By the boundedness of $a$, there exists a constant $C \ge 0$ with
\[
|a(u, v)| \le C \|u\|_1 \|v\|_1
\]
for all $u, v \in V$; hence
\[
|a(e, e - v_h)| \le C \|e\|_1 \|e - v_h\|_1.
\]
By coercivity there exists $c > 0$ with
\[
a(v, v) \ge c \|v\|_1^2
\]
for all $v \in V$; thus
\[
c \|e\|_1^2 \le |a(e, e)| \le C \|e\|_1 \|e - v_h\|_1
\]
for any $v_h \in V_h$. If $e \ne 0$, dividing by $\|e\|_1$ yields
\[
c \|e\|_1 \le C \|e - v_h\|_1.
\]
Taking the infimum over $v_h \in V_h$ gives
\[
c \|e\|_1 \le C \inf_{v_h \in V_h} \|e - v_h\|_1.
\]

Now observe that
\[
e - v_h = y - (y_h + v_h)
\]
and as $v_h$ runs over $V_h$, $w_h := y_h + v_h$ runs over $V_h$ as well;
hence
\[
\inf_{v_h \in V_h} \|e - v_h\|_1
=
\inf_{w_h \in V_h} \|y - w_h\|_1.
\]
Therefore we obtain the Céa inequality:
\[
\|y - y_h\|_1 \le \frac{C}{c} \inf_{w_h \in V_h} \|y - w_h\|_1.
\]

The constants $C$ and $c$ come from the boundedness and coercivity of $a$,
and the arguments rely on the standard well-posedness via Lax--Milgram for both
the continuous problem and its finite-dimensional Galerkin discretization, with
$f \in V'$ as the data. The result encapsulates that the discretization error
is bounded by a multiple of the best approximation error in $V_h$.

The proof is complete.
